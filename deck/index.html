Saudi Arabia's third sector hit its 2030 volunteering target six years early and multiplied its economic value by roughly nine.

Every headline figure is an input. Riyals committed. Organisations registered. Volunteers signed up.

Not one of them says what changed for anybody. The regulator has now shifted its own strategy from growing the sector to professionalising it — governance, standards, measurable impact.

The insight we are betting on

Carbon has a unit.
Social has none — so it
gets reported in proxies.

PillarCommon unitReported as
EnvironmentaltCO₂eAn outcome
GovernanceStructural factsVerifiable
SocialNoneTraining hours. People reached.

Training hours are an input. A policy is an intention. "People reached" is an activity wearing the costume of an outcome.

And the fix cannot be applied later. A programme that ran for three years without a baseline cannot be given one in year four.

Which means this is a delivery problem, not a reporting problem. That is the whole thesis.

What we are building

Torchbearer Verify

A tech-enabled platform that takes a social programme from design to audit-grade evidence — the measurement layer the sector is missing, delivered as software plus the field capability to make it real.

Design

Indicator builder

Programme designed against named SDG targets and Vision 2030 / SGI pillars, with baselines and instruments generated before delivery starts — because afterwards is too late.

Deliver

Field data capture

Offline-capable collection through local partners in the participant's own language, with attrition and exclusions recorded as they happen rather than reconstructed.

Verify

Evidence pack

Raw instruments, method notes, adjustments and reasoning, assembled so a third party can reconstruct the result without speaking to us. Formatted for disclosure and assurance.

06

The method the product encodes

Already written. Already
published. Not yet software.

Scope

Establish what is true before designing anything. Closes with a written problem statement and an explicit list of what will not be measurable.

Design

A causal chain with an indicator on every link, mapped to named external targets. Nothing is procured until cost per outcome is stated.

Deliver

Execution through local organisations and locally recruited teams, with data captured as the work happens.

Measure

Baseline at intake, endline at a fixed interval, same instrument, same population. Attrition published as a number.

Verify

An evidence trail complete enough that a sceptical reader can disagree with our conclusion using our own data.

Business model

Revenue from day one.
Not a grant pipeline.

Land

Design engagement

Fixed-scope: programme design, measurement plan, indicator mapping, cost-per-outcome model. Stands alone — the client is free to deliver it with anyone. Establishes the relationship and the standard.

Expand

Platform subscription

Per-programme licence for Verify: instrument builder, field capture, evidence pack generation. The recurring line, and the reason this is a venture rather than a consultancy.

Deepen

Delivery & verification

Full execution through local partners, or independent verification of programmes already running. Higher value, and the source of the field data that keeps the product honest.

The wedge is the design engagement: small, fixed-price, and it produces the artefact that makes the platform obviously necessary. Nobody buys measurement software until they have seen what their current numbers cannot survive.
