Circular economy

Reusables only reduce waste if people keep using them

3 March 2026 · Method paper · 8 min

Life-cycle evidence favours reusables on nearly every impact — except when they are airfreighted, at which point they are worse than what they replace. And displacement modelled on rated lifespan assumes a continued use that water, drying space and household acceptance decide.
Measurement

Why most impact numbers do not survive an audit

19 February 2026 · Method paper · 9 min

No baseline, attrition absorbed into the result, attribution claimed without a comparison, and reach reported in place of outcome. Four failure modes, four questions that expose them, and why this is becoming a commercial problem rather than an ethical one.
Women's health

The school days nobody counts

22 January 2026 · Pakistan · 9 min

In 25,305 women surveyed across one district of rural Sindh, 61.9% used old cloth and 12.6% used nothing at all. Cost was the barrier for 48.7% — awareness for 12.1%. Most menstrual health programming is weighted towards the constraint that binds least.
