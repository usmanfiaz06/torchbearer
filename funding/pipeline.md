# Torchbearer: development funding pipeline

Researched 17 August 2026. Everything below was checked against the funder's own material or
current reporting, and each entry says plainly whether Torchbearer is eligible today.

Read the last section first. Two structural issues decide whether most of this list is reachable at
all, and both are fixable.

---

## The thing that changed, and that everyone's old contact list still gets wrong

**USAID no longer exists.** It closed on 1 July 2025 and its remaining functions moved to the State
Department. US disbursements fell from about USD 68 billion in 2024 to about USD 32 billion in 2025.
Any funding template, consultant list or partner introduction that routes through USAID is dead
paper. The knock-on matters too: a lot of INGOs that would previously have sub-granted to Pakistani
delivery partners lost the prime contracts that funded those sub-grants, so the intermediary layer is
thinner than it was two years ago.

Practical consequence: the money that remains is concentrated in European bilaterals, UN pooled
funds, Gulf philanthropy, and prize or fellowship capital. That is where this list points.

## Tier 1: apply, with a real chance

### GEF Small Grants Programme, Pakistan (UNDP)
- **Amount:** typically USD 25,000 to 50,000, maximum USD 75,000
- **Fit:** strong. Community-led environment and climate work. Pakistan is a mature SGP country
  programme, upgraded to a full-sized project managed by UNDP.
- **Eligibility:** NGOs and community-based organisations. **Check whether a private limited company
  qualifies before writing anything** — this is the recurring blocker, see the last section.
- **Timing:** an OP8 window has a 15 September 2026 deadline. Confirm the exact Pakistan country
  programme date with the UNDP Islamabad office rather than trusting an aggregator.
- **Why it is first on the list:** the amount matches the size of work you can actually evidence, and
  a completed SGP grant is the reference that unlocks the bigger funds.
- sgp.undp.org

### Zayed Sustainability Prize 2028 cycle
- **Amount:** USD 1 million per category winner
- **Categories that fit:** Health, Climate Action, Water
- **Eligibility:** SMEs and non-profits, explicitly. A private limited company is fine here.
- **Timing:** the 2027 cycle ran January to June 2026 and has closed. The next call opens around
  January 2027. Put it in the calendar now.
- **Why it matters beyond the money:** it is a Gulf platform. Winning or shortlisting does more for
  Saudi market entry than the cash does.
- zayedsustainabilityprize.com

### Cartier Women's Initiative, 2028 edition
- **Amount:** USD 100,000 first place, 60,000 second, 30,000 third, per regional award
- **Region:** there is a dedicated South Asia and Central Asia award
- **Eligibility:** women-owned and women-led impact businesses. Torchbearer qualifies on the criterion
  most applicants struggle with.
- **Timing:** the 2027 edition opened 16 April 2026 and closed 16 June 2026, so it has just been
  missed. The next call should open around April 2027.
- **Requirement to prepare for:** English at CEFR B2 or above, and demonstrated impact already
  realised. That second one is where the missing programme figures bite.
- cartierwomensinitiative.com

### Islamic Development Bank, Transform Fund
- **Amount:** grants of USD 50,000 to 150,000; equity participation up to USD 1 million
- **Fit:** good, and underused by Pakistani applicants. Pakistan is an IsDB member country. The fund
  is framed around science, technology and innovation, which suits Torchbearer Verify far better than
  it suits a delivery programme.
- **Eligibility:** explicitly includes businesses, NGOs and academic institutions
- **Route:** the ENGAGE portal, isdb-engage.org
- **Angle:** pitch the measurement platform, not the menstrual health programme. This is a technology
  fund and the OIC framing works in your favour.

### Canada Fund for Local Initiatives, Pakistan
- **Amount:** average CAD 30,000, maximum CAD 100,000
- **Fit:** very strong on paper. Its three thematic priorities are environment and climate action,
  inclusive governance, and gender equality and the empowerment of women and girls. Torchbearer sits
  on two of the three.
- **Eligibility:** designed for small local civil society organisations, projects conceived by local
  partners, delivered in Pakistan
- **Timing:** annual, typically opening in the first quarter with a deadline around May
- **Action now:** email ISBAD-CFLI-FCIL@international.gc.ca to ask to be notified when the next call
  opens. Embassy funds reward being known before the call.

### Karandaaz Pakistan
- **Vehicles:** Innovation Challenge Fund, and Women Ventures
- **Funded by:** UK aid and the Gates Foundation
- **Fit:** strong on women-led enterprise and financial inclusion. Aimen's Mobilink Bank work sits
  directly in Karandaaz's world, which matters more than the form does.
- **Timing:** periodic calls rather than a standing window. Monitor.
- karandaaz.com.pk

### Pakistan Startup Fund (Ignite, Ministry of IT)
- **Amount:** 10% to 30% of a round, equity-free, no board seat
- **Timing:** rolling, no deadline, no application fee
- **Catch:** it co-funds alongside a private venture capital lead. It is not standalone capital. Only
  relevant once Torchbearer Verify is raising a priced round.

## Tier 2: larger, slower, and worth preparing for rather than rushing

### Adaptation Fund Climate Innovation Accelerator (AFCIA)
- **Amount:** small grants up to USD 250,000
- **Channelled through:** UNDP and UNEP, USD 5 million each
- **Fit:** good. Explicitly targets NGOs, community groups and young innovators, and explicitly funds
  innovation in adaptation. Your flood-response and menstrual-health-in-displacement work is an
  adaptation story that almost nobody else is telling.

### Grand Challenges Canada
- **Amount:** CAD 150,000 to 1.5 million depending on stage
- **Pakistan is one of twelve named priority countries.** That is a significant advantage.
- **Eligibility:** must be legally incorporated and able to administer funds. A private limited
  company is acceptable; individuals and sole proprietorships are not.
- **Timing:** the window that closed 31 March 2026 is done. Watch grandchallenges.ca/apply-for-funding
  for the next call.

### Echoing Green Fellowship
- **Amount:** USD 100,000 stipend plus support, to the founder rather than the organisation
- **Fit:** designed for exactly Aimen's profile and stage
- **Timing:** applications open around September and close in early October each year. The 2026 cycle
  closed 8 October 2025, so the next opens in about a month.
- **This is the nearest deadline on the whole list after SGP.** echoinggreen.org/fellowship/apply

### Sanitation and Hygiene Fund (SHF)
- **The single best thematic match in existence.** A UN fund dedicated to sanitation, hygiene and
  menstrual health, whose Capital M initiative exists specifically to build menstrual health markets.
  Funded by the Netherlands, Switzerland and the Gates Foundation, targeting USD 2 billion over five
  years.
- **How it actually works:** catalytic financing through country programmes and blended finance, not
  an open application portal. You reach it by being the delivery partner a country programme needs.
- **Action:** track which countries have live SHF programmes and whether Pakistan is among them, and
  approach as a partner rather than an applicant.
- shfund.org

### UN Trust Fund to End Violence against Women and Girls
- **Amount:** up to USD 20 million allocated across cohorts
- **Timing:** concept notes September to November annually
- **Honest assessment: weak fit and brutal odds.** The last call drew 3,925 applications from 128
  countries requesting USD 2.1 billion. Eligibility requires the organisation to be focused on ending
  violence against women and girls. Torchbearer is not, and stretching a menstrual health programme
  into a VAWG frame is the kind of thing reviewers spot immediately. Skip unless you build a genuine
  VAWG programme.

### Global Innovation Fund
- Open to social enterprises and for-profits in any developing country, which is unusually permissive.
- Its last application window closed in January 2025. Verify current status before investing time.

## Tier 3: Gulf and Saudi, where the strategy already points

The important thing to understand: **Gulf philanthropy is relationship-led, not application-led.**
There is rarely a portal. Budget your effort accordingly.

### KSrelief (King Salman Humanitarian Aid and Relief Centre)
- 2026 plan: 113 projects across 44 countries, in partnership with 11 Saudi non-profits, plus UN
  agencies and vetted international and local organisations
- **Route in:** as an implementing partner to a Saudi non-profit or a UN agency already inside a
  KSrelief programme. Not a direct grant application.
- Pakistan is a recurring KSrelief geography, which is the opening.

### Misk Foundation, with the Gates Foundation
- A partnership supporting non-profits and social enterprises in the Kingdom
- Requires Saudi presence, which is the point of the Launchpad application

### Alwaleed Philanthropies
- Large, active in health and disaster relief, first philanthropic contributor to the UN Multi-Partner
  Human Security Trust Fund
- **No open call.** Invitation and relationship only.

## What actually blocks you, and what to do about it

Everything above is reachable. Two things currently stand between Torchbearer and most of it.

### 1. Legal form

Torchbearer Pvt Ltd is a private limited company. A significant share of institutional grant funding
requires non-profit status, and in Pakistan that means a Section 42 company under the Companies Act
2017, or a registered trust or society, usually with a Pakistan Centre for Philanthropy certification
for the larger donors.

The funds that accept a company as it stands: Zayed, Cartier, IsDB Transform, Grand Challenges Canada,
Pakistan Startup Fund, Karandaaz, and every investor in Tier 4.

The funds that will probably require a non-profit vehicle: GEF SGP, CFLI, UN pooled funds, and most
bilateral money.

**Recommendation:** register a Section 42 entity alongside the company. It is the standard structure
for exactly this situation, a social enterprise that needs to take both grant and commercial capital,
and it roughly doubles the addressable list. Budget three to six months.

### 2. Programme figures

Almost every application asks the same four things: how many people, in which districts, over what
dates, with which delivery partners. Torchbearer does not currently publish any of them, and the
website says so honestly rather than inventing them.

That honesty is an asset in the room and a liability in the form. Cartier requires demonstrated impact
already realised. SGP requires a defined community and site. CFLI requires a named local partner.

**Recommendation:** before anything else on this list, assemble the Maahwari record. Participants,
districts, dates, partners, kits distributed, any follow-up done. Even rough figures with a stated
method beat no figures, and you already argue publicly that a bounded number honestly described is
worth more than a large one that is not. That argument only works if there is a number.
